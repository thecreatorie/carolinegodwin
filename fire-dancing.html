Fire Dancing

Licensed and insured fire performance at its most primal — fans, hoops, staffs, fire breathing and eating, and a one-of-a-kind fire skirt.

Book Fire Dancing

Caroline has been fire dancing since 2015. She is a sponsored performer for the fire performance companies Juggling Calling and Pyrotechnique Las Vegas, runs the Vegas Fire Dancers community on Instagram, and organizes the weekly Las Vegas Fire Jams.

Book fire as a choreographed feature, as ambient fire flow through your event, or combined with aerial in the Fire Aerial Pentagram and Fire Lyra with lycopodium bursts.
