Teaching Circus & Flow

Weekly classes, private lessons, and festival workshops in aerial, flow arts, pole, and fire — for every level.

Book Now

Caroline teaches weekly classes at Pole Fitness Studio, Las Vegas — flexibility, aerial hoop, aerial hammock, and flow arts — and has taught workshops at flow festivals across the country, including Firedrums, Flowstorm, Southwest Flow Fest, and June Jam.

Private lessons and group workshops are available for team-building sessions, festival workshops, and specialized classes.
