Book Stiltwalking

Stilt walkers turn heads from across the room. Caroline performs on both peg stilts and drywall stilts, bringing costumed characters that roam and interact with guests, or stop for a featured flow or fire moment.

Looks range from glowing LED wings and fans to fully costumed characters — and Caroline makes her own custom stilt covers, so every look is built by her.
