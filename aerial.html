Aerial

Book Aerial

Caroline began her aerial journey in 2018 and soon moved to Las Vegas to take her circus career to the next level. Her most recognizable act is the Fire Aerial Pentagram — a custom five-point star apparatus, rigged and lit ablaze while she performs inside it.

Every apparatus is available as a polished choreographed feature or as ambient performance through the night, and rigging and aerial points can be provided.
